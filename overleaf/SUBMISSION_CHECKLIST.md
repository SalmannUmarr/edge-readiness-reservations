# Final Overleaf submission checklist

The final research article is `main.tex`. The earlier proposal is preserved as
`proposal_legacy.tex`; do not set it as the primary document.

## Upload and compile

1. Create or open the group Overleaf project.
2. Upload `main.tex` and the complete `figures/` folder without changing their
   relative paths.
3. In Overleaf **Menu > Main document**, select `main.tex`.
4. Compile with pdfLaTeX. The class is the prescribed two-column
   `IEEEtran` conference format.
5. Confirm that the resulting article is 8--11 pages inclusive. If pagination
   differs, adjust figure placement or concise prose only; do not remove results,
   limitations, DOI references, or reproducibility information.
